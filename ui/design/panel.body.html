Marble is the room, not the furniture

It sits behind the page. Numbers, tables and forms always get a flat opaque surface. No blur, no glow, no translucent panels.

One loud thing per screen

The verdict line on the split screen. The headline number on results. Everything else is 13 to 15px.

One primary button per view

Solid pill for the action that moves the demo forward. Everything else is outline or text.

Shape before colour

Outcome pills read in greyscale and on a projector. Colour is only for diffs and grading.

Show the receipt

Every row carries its tier and cost. Every claim carries a citation chip that opens the record.

Plain copy

Sentences a controller would say. No em dashes, no exclamation marks, no agent theatrics.
